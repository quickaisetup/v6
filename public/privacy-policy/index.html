Effective from 18 November 2026 · Version 2.0

This Privacy Policy explains how QUICKAISETUP LTD (“Quick AI Setup”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you visit www.quickaisetup.com, contact us, use our services, or interact with a business that uses our services. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, and the requirements of the messaging carriers we use. A Hungarian version is available: Adatkezelési tájékoztató.

3. Use of artificial intelligence

In line with the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), we tell you that our chatbots, AI phone receptionist and review-reply suggestions are powered by artificial intelligence. When you chat with or call one of these systems you are talking to an AI, not a person, and we say so at the start or on screen. You can ask for a human at any time. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you (GDPR Art. 22), and we do not profile you.

5. SMS / text messaging

We only send text messages to people who have expressly agreed to receive them, for example by ticking a separate, unticked SMS checkbox on a form, or by texting us first. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

  • Message frequency varies (booking confirmations, reminders, replies to your questions, offers).
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help, or email quickaisetup@gmail.com.
No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from all of the sharing described in this policy and will not be shared with any third parties. Phone numbers are only passed to the providers needed to deliver the messages (such as LC Phone / Twilio and mobile carriers).

7. International transfers

We are based in the United Kingdom, which the European Commission recognises as providing adequate protection. HighLevel and several of its sub-processors process data in the United States. These transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (and its UK Extension) where the provider is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses with the UK Addendum / IDTA, plus supplementary measures such as encryption and access controls. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

8. Data we process for our clients

When a gym, salon, café or hotel (our client) uses our system to talk to its customers, that business is the controller of its customers’ data. We process that data only on the client’s documented instructions under a data processing agreement (GDPR Art. 28), solely to provide the service, with HighLevel acting as our sub-processor. This typically includes names, phone numbers, emails, messages, bookings, membership and payment details, reviews and call transcripts. To exercise your rights over this data, please contact the business you dealt with; if you contact us, we will forward your request and help them respond. The client decides how long data is kept; when a contract ends we return or delete the data as the client chooses.

9. Cookies and our website

www.quickaisetup.com does not use tracking or advertising cookies. We only store your chosen language in your browser (localStorage). Our host (Vercel) keeps short-lived technical logs (IP address, time, page requested) for security, based on legitimate interest. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google’s servers. If we add analytics or advertising pixels in the future, we will ask for your consent first via a cookie banner. Pages run on HighLevel (booking pages, chat widgets, forms) may use their own functional cookies, which are explained on those pages.

10. Security

  • Access is limited to authorised staff using accounts protected by two-factor authentication.
  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • We choose providers for their security guarantees and sign data processing terms with them.
  • We review access regularly and delete data we no longer need.
